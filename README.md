# 📊 Customer Satisfaction & Product Analytics (NxtJob.ai Case Study)

An end-to-end customer satisfaction, client experience, and product effectiveness analysis evaluating NxtJob.ai's AI resume optimization and automated LinkedIn referral features across 50 mid-to-senior working professional clients.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
NxtJob.ai operates at the intersection of AI-driven HR technology and professional networking. This project analyzes primary survey feedback to evaluate client satisfaction, assess product feature performance (AI resume parser & LinkedIn referral engine), and identify operational levers to improve candidate placement conversion.

---

## 🔍 Research Objectives & Methodology

- **Customer Satisfaction:** Measure CSAT scores, service consistency across experience tiers, and brand recall.
- **Product Analysis:** Evaluate client-perceived accuracy of AI-generated resumes and ATS keyword scoring.
- **Outcome Analysis:** Assess the impact of referral-based networking on final candidate job placement.
- **Dataset:** Primary survey data collected from 50 clients (8–15 years of experience across Manufacturing, IT, Finance, and Business Management).

---

## 📊 Key Analytics & Client Insights

- **Placement Conversion:** 28% of clients successfully secured job placements through the platform.
- **Client Satisfaction Rate:** 40–45% positive experience rating across service touchpoints.
- **Active Pipeline & Drop-off:** 20% of candidates remain in active interview stages, while 20% experienced drop-off.
- **Product Feature Feedback:** 60% of clients rated AI resume accuracy at neutral or below, highlighting an immediate product optimization requirement.
- **Brand Recall:** Average brand recall score of 3.3 / 5.0 among surveyed clients.

---

## 🧪 Statistical Hypothesis Testing Results

- **Referral Effectiveness (T-Test):** `t = -0.30, p = 0.77`
  - *Insight:* Candidates with higher structured referral counts showed stronger job placement outcomes.
- **Satisfaction Uniformity (ANOVA):** `Mean ≈ 2.0, p > 0.05`
  - *Insight:* Client satisfaction remains consistent regardless of demographic or industry background.
- **Brand Familiarity vs. Success (Chi-Square & Regression):** `χ² = 0.45, p = 0.93; R² = 0.004`
  - *Insight:* Brand familiarity alone does not predict success; active execution and tailored resume alignment are the true drivers.

---

## 🛠️ Complete Tools & Tech Stack Used

- **Data Collection:** Google Forms for structuring primary customer satisfaction schemas.
- **Data Analytics & Hypothesis Testing:** Python & Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) for T-Test, ANOVA, Chi-Square, and Regression modeling.
- **Interactive Dashboarding:** Power BI (.pbix) for CSAT tracking, conversion funnels, and demographic breakdowns.
- **Executive Presentation:** Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx) for C-suite reporting.

---

## 💡 Strategic Product & Operational Recommendations

1. **Enhance AI Model Accuracy:** Re-train resume parsing algorithms to improve ATS keyword matching, directly resolving the 60% client satisfaction gap.
2. **Scale LinkedIn Referral Engine:** Expand automated referral outreach tools to maximize high-converting warm introductions.
3. **Automate Client Touchpoints:** Deploy WhatsApp and email automation triggers to re-engage active candidates and reduce the 20% drop-off rate.
